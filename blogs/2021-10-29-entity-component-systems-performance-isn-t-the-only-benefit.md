Many companies, like [Unity](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.entities@0.17/manual/index.html) and [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/General/Conceptual/GameplayKit_Guide/EntityComponent.html#//apple_ref/doc/uid/TP40015172-CH6-SW1) are using Entity Component Systems (ECS) to make games because having tightly packed data leads to cache efficiency and great performance. ECS isn’t just great for performance though: it leads to good code-decoupling, easy composition, and lends itself to TDD and automated testing.
