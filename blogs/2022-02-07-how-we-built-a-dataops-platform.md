[DataOps](https://dataopsmanifesto.org/en/) has always been the vision for the ClassDojo team, even if we didn’t think of it using that term. Our goal has been to give every vertically integrated team ownership over its own metrics, tables, and data dictionaries, and the processes for generating those artifacts. These things should be written mostly in code, with some generated documentation.
